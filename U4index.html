One line, two forms

Formula guide · slope, point-slope, HOY & VUX

Connect the forms

m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁)
y − y₁ = m(x − x₁)
y = mx + b   ·   b = y₁ − mx₁

Slope is signed rise ÷ signed run. Use a point on the line in point-slope form. The y-intercept is (0, b).

HOY: horizontal, zero, y

m = 0   ·   y = k

Horizontal lines keep the same y-coordinate. Their rise is 0. Point-slope form is y − k = 0(x − x₁); slope-intercept form simplifies to y = k.

VUX: vertical, undefined, x

m is undefined   ·   x = k

Vertical lines keep the same x-coordinate. Their run is 0, and division by zero is undefined. They have no point-slope or slope-intercept form and are not functions of x.
